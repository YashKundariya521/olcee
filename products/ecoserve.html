EcoServe® Biodegradable Tableware

Sustainable Dining Solutions

EcoServe® is Olcee International's premier line of biodegradable and compostable tableware. Made from renewable resources, our products are designed to reduce environmental impact without compromising on quality or functionality. Ideal for parties, events, catering, and everyday use.

Key Features:

  • Made from plant-based materials (e.g., sugarcane bagasse, PLA).
  • 100% biodegradable and compostable.
  • Sturdy and durable, suitable for hot and cold foods.
  • Microwave and freezer safe (specifics vary by product).
  • Available in various sizes and types: plates, bowls, cups, cutlery.
