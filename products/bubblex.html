BubbleX® Bubble Wrap

Superior Cushioning for Fragile Items

BubbleX® by Olcee International is your go-to solution for protecting delicate and valuable items. Our bubble wrap offers reliable cushioning against shocks, vibrations, and abrasions during transit and storage.

Key Features:

  • Lightweight and flexible, conforming to various shapes.
  • Durable bubbles that retain air for long-lasting protection.
  • Available in different bubble sizes (e.g., small, medium, large).
  • Options include standard, anti-static, and biodegradable bubble wrap.
  • Perforated options for easy tearing.
  • Wide range of roll widths and lengths.
