Flexifoil® Stretch Films

Secure Your Shipments with Confidence

Flexifoil® by Olcee International offers a range of high-quality stretch films designed for superior load containment and protection. Our films ensure your products remain secure and intact during handling, transit, and storage.

Key Features:

  • Excellent puncture and tear resistance.
  • High clarity for easy barcode scanning and load identification.
  • Consistent cling and unwind performance.
  • Available in various gauges, widths, and lengths.
  • Types: Hand wrap, machine wrap, colored, UV-resistant.
  • Made from LLDPE (Linear Low-Density Polyethylene).
