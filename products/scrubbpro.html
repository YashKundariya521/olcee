ScrubbPro® Scrubber Making Machines

Efficient Scrubber Manufacturing Solutions

ScrubbPro® machines from Olcee International are engineered for high-efficiency production of various types of scrubbers, including steel scrubbers, mesh scrubbers, and sponge scrubbers. Built for durability and performance, these machines are ideal for businesses looking to scale their scrubber manufacturing operations.

Key Features:

  • Robust construction for long operational life.
  • High production capacity with consistent quality.
  • Easy to operate and maintain.
  • Adjustable settings for different scrubber sizes and types.
  • Models available for various levels of automation (manual, semi-automatic, fully automatic).
  • Energy-efficient designs.
